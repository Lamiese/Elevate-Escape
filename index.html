Step 02

Booking travel

As a travel agent you have multiple ways to search and book travel. Don't feel like you need to master all of them straight away, start with one or two and build from there.

Important booking reminder: You will learn this in your training but please remember: anything booked outside of the InteleTravel platform, whether it's with a partner or a hotel direct, that is commissionable, must be registered in your back office immediately.

▶ Your agent rate spreadsheet

One of the biggest perks of being a travel agent is access to heavily discounted personal travel rates at hundreds of hotels worldwide: up to 80% off at some properties. These rates are for you, your friends and family.

The spreadsheet has two tabs: Hotels (every partner with the discount, how to register and which destinations are covered) and Search by Destination (find which partners cover a specific country instantly).

▶ Booking direct with hotels

You can approach hotels directly to book at commissionable rates: but there is a process that must be followed. This is not the same as booking through a comparison site like Skyscanner or Booking.com.

  • Identify yourself as an InteleTravel agent
  • Provide InteleTravel's IATA number: 10-548020
  • Ask for a commissionable rate
  • Get written confirmation of the commission percentage
  • Collect all information to pass to your client
  • Take payment directly (ensure client has completed a payment authorisation form)
  • Register the booking in your InteleTravel system
  • Once travel is complete, submit a ticket in InteleTravel Hub: ticket type: Invoice Request Bookings

Email template: requesting a commissionable rate:

Hi [Hotel Name],

My name is [Your Name] and I am an Independent Travel Agent with InteleTravel. Our IATA number is 10-548020.

Please could you provide me with your best commissionable rate for [number of guests] arriving on [date] for [number of nights] on [board basis] and confirm the commission percentage attached to this rate.

Thank you so much in advance. I look forward to hearing from you.

[Your professional signature]
Step 03

Important UK booking rules

Please read this carefully before you start booking for clients.

As a UK travel agent registered under InteleTravel's UK licence, you can only book travel for clients who live and work in the UK. You cannot book for UK citizens living abroad, even if they have a UK address or passport. This is a compliance rule: not a guideline. When in doubt, ask before you book.

Prospecting

How to find people

Your ideal person is you: someone who loves travel, wants flexibility, or is open to extra income. Keep that in mind with every conversation.

▶ Offline prospecting

Start with your warm market: friends, family, colleagues, gym friends. Then networking events, local markets, business meetups, and chatting to people who offer you a service. Business cards and flyers work well if you're out regularly.

▶ Online prospecting: Instagram

Instagram is your biggest tool. Be consistent, show up daily and niche down your search. Think about where your ideal person hangs out online: travel accounts, lifestyle bloggers, local restaurant pages, corporate job hashtags, mum communities.

▶ Attraction marketing

People buy you before they buy the business. Your social media needs to show who you are, not just what you do.

  • Document don't create: show your journey as it happens
  • Share your story and why you started
  • Show lifestyle content alongside business content
  • Be consistent: showing up matters more than being perfect
